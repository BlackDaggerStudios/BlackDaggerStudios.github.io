Who we are

Black Dagger Studios is a small, independent team from Poland making games on Roblox. We started in 2026 and we are building our first game right now.

We like games with a story, a dark atmosphere and missions you remember. That is the kind of game we want to make.

A small request

We are a new studio and still learning. Our first releases will not be perfect, and some things will take time. If you play our games, tell us what works and what does not. That is how we will get better.

Operation Black Dagger

Our first game is a story-driven military shooter set in a fictional modern conflict. An elite special forces unit is sent behind enemy lines to steal a secret document. Not everything goes to plan.

Play the campaign solo with an AI squad, or bring a friend in co-op.
